# 4×5 TFIM PEPO and MPO DMT probe

The 20-qubit first-order Trotter circuit was simulated with an independent
state vector and compared with ordinary MPO DMT and experimental PEPO DMT.
The reproducible driver is [`examples/pepo_dmt_ising_4x5.py`](../../../examples/pepo_dmt_ising_4x5.py).

$$
H=-\sum_{\langle i,j\rangle}Z_i Z_j-0.7\sum_i X_i,
\qquad \rho_0=|+\rangle\!\langle+|^{\otimes 20},\quad \Delta t=0.05.
$$

The 4×5 lattice has open boundaries. Each timestep applies all 31 ZZ gates,
then all 20 X gates. The vector reference applies **the same Trotter gates**;
it does not assess Trotter error against $e^{-iHt}$. Both DMT implementations
protect only the central L $\{(1,1),(1,2),(2,1)\}$. All 48 L-shaped densities
are independently read out against the state vector. MPO uses snake-row-major
mapping, paper truncation and zero cutoff. PEPO uses direct boundary MPS with
`boundary_chi=8`, `chi=4` and an eight-million-entry map cap.

## One timestep

Absolute Frobenius errors in reduced density matrices; single-run wall times
include optimizer gate replay but exclude reference and independent readout.
The environment used NumPy complex128 and `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1`. Times are
not comparable across bond dimensions or a general performance claim.

| Method | Bond cap | Protected L error | Max error over all L | Replay time |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| MPO DMT | 8 | 0.00520 | 0.0701 | 0.60 s |
| MPO DMT | 16 | 0.000348 | 0.00521 | 1.06 s |
| MPO DMT | 32 | 0.000418 | 0.00546 | 2.63 s |
| PEPO DMT | 4 | 8.6e-15 | 1.1e-14 | 0.18 s |

PEPO maximum reported pair error was 2.6e-15, so this first-timestep PEPO
result is effectively **untruncated**; it is not evidence of lossy-DMT
accuracy. MPO caps measure a different network topology and storage budget.
The MPO per-split constraint residual was below 9e-15 at chi 8, despite a
larger error relative to the original exact trajectory after later gates.
The nonmonotonic 16/32 result is an observation, not a convergence theorem.

## Lossy second timestep

For the first gate of the second timestep, construct an independent untruncated
gate target from the current PEPO and compare exact regional contractions:
the protected L changed by 9.3e-11 and the largest unprotected L by 8.3e-6.
The reported local pair error was 5.3e-5. These are **single-compression**
errors and differ from errors against the complete reference trajectory.

With `dmt_refine=False`, two complete timesteps (102 gates) finished. PEPO
selected-L/max-all-L errors versus the state-vector circuit were
5.39e-5/0.0136, with max reported per-gate map residual 6.18e-9; the replay
took 8.84 s. MPO chi 8 selected/max errors were 0.0235/0.135 and took 1.19 s.
Skipping refinement preserves its strict computed-map tolerance but can worsen
the global approximation. The PEPO result shows that a preserved marginal at
each split need not stay equal to the exact long-time trajectory.

Across the 48 final L panels, MPO/PEPO maximum trace errors were
1.5e-14/2.7e-8, maximum Hermiticity defects were 0.0539/0.000854, and minimum
eigenvalues of the *Hermitian parts* were -0.0386/-5.79e-5. The panels are
not necessarily Hermitian; these eigenvalues do not certify positivity. They
show that neither method guarantees physically valid local densities here.

With refinement enabled, one probe took 83 s by the tenth gate of timestep
two and was interrupted during the next refinement. The fast option should not
be presented as an equal-quality replacement for objective refinement.

## Limits and implementation findings

The all-48-L PEPO run with a one-million-entry cap hit the regional-map guard
at the 18th ZZ gate of the first timestep; the one-L experiment above used a
larger cap. The prototype explicitly materializes maps, does not share strips
across different L regions, and the finite boundary environment does not
guarantee exact physical preservation. PEPO results here use CPU NumPy only.

The experiment exposed a premature feasibility stop: the optimizer's gradient
criterion could succeed while the actual regional residual was above tolerance.
The solver now retries with tighter stopping thresholds within its existing
evaluation budget and retains the per-region acceptance check. The map budget
is checked for all requested regions before allocating any of their maps.

Reproduce the two measurements from the Pepsy root:

```bash
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python examples/pepo_dmt_ising_4x5.py --mpo-chi 8 --probe-lossy
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python examples/pepo_dmt_ising_4x5.py --mpo-chi 8 --steps 2 --no-refine
```

An independent one-gate reference check is in
[`tests/test_pepo_ising_example.py`](../../../tests/test_pepo_ising_example.py).
