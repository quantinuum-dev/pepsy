## Why

- The `fh_mps` notebook showed a mismatch between local-term energy and direct
  MPO energy after imaginary-time evolution on a periodic mapped lattice.
- The root cause was a convention bridge issue: native fermionic Symmray MPS
  tensors were being compared with a bosonic JW MPO without the full JW endpoint
  and MPS bond-sector phase convention.

- `pytest -q tests/test_symmetric_tensors.py` -> 84 passed.

- A 4 by 3 periodic `fh_mps`-shaped scratch run matched term and MPO energies
  through the first two imaginary-time schedule blocks:

  - after `tau=0.1`, 10 steps: about `-2.6e-15`
  - after `tau=0.03`, 10 steps: about `-6.1e-16`

## Decisions / findings

- For an edge `i < j`, the finite-chain JW MPO uses endpoint parity on the left
  side:
  - forward channel: `c_i^dag P_i ... P_{j-1} c_j`
  - Hermitian-conjugate channel: `P_i c_i ... P_{j-1} c_j^dag`
  - coefficient: `-t_sigma`
- No site-0 parity prefix and no alternating `(-1)^i` sign patch are used.
- `_bosonize_fermionic_tn(...)` must preserve Symmray lazy phases and apply the
  MPS bond-sector phase generated by the native fermionic contraction.
- DMRG is still deferred; the current milestone is only that MPS/MPO energies
  agree reliably.
