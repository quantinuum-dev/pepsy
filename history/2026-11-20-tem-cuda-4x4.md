# 2026-11-20 — CUDA error mitigation on a 4×4 Ising torus

On `develop` at `a5fb031`, the local `MpoErrorMit` changes accept dense Torch
MPS trajectories and place the mitigation MPO and shot contractions on a
Torch device through `to_backend`. The tested Quimb build uses a direct CUDA
warm start for DMRG2. The small dense reference exercises mixed NumPy and
Torch CPU/CUDA in a single process.

The depth-10 benchmark uses the intermediate-temperature quench on a
periodic 4×4 lattice. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.20870)
It applies a two-qubit depolarizing channel with Pauli fault probability
0.00075 after every RZZ gate. Exact 16-qubit Trotter-step targets feed CUDA
MPS projection at χ=32, 64, and 128. The 600 shots per cap share their sampled
faults and bases. The second stage builds three CUDA DMRG2 mitigation MPOs at
χ=8, 16, and 32 and scores nested 150-, 300-, and 600-shot subsets.

The [local run summary](../store/tfim-tem-cuda-4x4-20261120.38kCIX/summary.json)
indexes the saved shots, MPO cores, exact references, and combined estimates.
It records the trace row alongside scale-invariant local overlap and labels
the optional trace-ratio diagnostic. The gate-by-gate MPS fidelity calibration
and its superseded shot run remain in the local logs.

MPS χ=128 gave 0.99673 final noiseless fidelity and a pre-readout second
magnetization moment of 0.52104 on the sampled noisy paths. Exact noisy
replay gave 0.51896 on the same paths. The MPO identity coefficient fell from
1.20644 at χ=8 to 1.15293 at χ=32. The χ=128/32 case at 600 shots gave a raw
second moment of 0.55163, a mitigated value of 0.66262, and a trace ratio of
0.57471. The noiseless value was 0.57511.

The TEM, public API, and package layout selection passed 73 tests, including
the CUDA regression. Ruff and `git diff --check` passed. The changes are in the
working tree.
